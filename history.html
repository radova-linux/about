History

From one idea to one system.

How Radova Linux grew from a personal project in 2019 to its public release in 2026.

  1. A personal project

    Jakarta, 1 Januari 2018

    Radova began with one simple idea: an operating system that is fast, secure, and usable by information institutions at every level.

  2. Research

    Jakarta, 1 Januari 2022–2024

    Bai and null led the research into what an operating system for information management really needs.

    • BBai
    • Nnull

  3. Built in-house

    Jakarta, 1 Januari 2024

    An internal development team began building Radova, and Haidar and Sulthan ran its first internal trials.

    • HHaidar
    • SSulthan

  4. Developed in the open

    Jakarta, 1 Januari 2025

    Haidar and Sulthan officially joined the team, and Radova moved to open, public development.

  5. Kautsar joined

    Jakarta, 1 Januari 2026

    Kautsar joined the Radova team.

    • Kautsar

  6. Released

    Jakarta, 1 Januari 2026

    Radova Linux was officially released.
